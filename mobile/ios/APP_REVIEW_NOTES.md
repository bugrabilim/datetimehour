Attached. The app has no account registration or login, no user-generated content and no paid content or in-app purchases, so none of those flows exist.

2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Saat Tarih ("Time & Date") is a free clock and calendar utility. It answers everyday questions such as "what time is it, what is today's date, when is the next prayer time, when is the next public holiday, what time is it in another city?" in one app.
Target audience: everyday users in Turkey and Turkish/Arabic/German/Azerbaijani speakers worldwide, especially people who follow daily prayer times and Turkey's official holiday calendar.
Value: a large clock with 19 clock faces, today's date and week number, prayer times for any location calculated on the device (with optional reminders and a Ramadan timetable image), a calendar with Turkey's official and religious holidays (and, optionally, the user's own calendars), world clocks with a spinning globe, weather, seasons/sun/moon information written for children, and tools (alarm, timer and countdown, stopwatch, pomodoro, date calculator). There are no ads and no tracking.

3. HOW TO USE / ACCESS THE MAIN FEATURES
No login, account or sample files are needed. Everything works right after launch.
- Clock tab: swipe left/right to change the clock face (1/19 counter at the bottom; tap the counter for a list). Tap the clock to show/hide seconds. "Edit" chooses the items under the clock. The full-screen button shows the clock full screen.
- Location: Istanbul is used by default. Tap the location name (Prayer, Weather or World tabs, or Settings) to pick one of Turkey's 81 provinces or "Use my location" (asks for location permission only at that moment).
- Weather tab: current conditions, hourly and 7-day forecast for the selected location.
- Calendar tab: month view with holidays; tap a day for details. "Show my phone's calendars" asks for calendar access (optional). "+" adds an event with the system editor.
- World tab: spinning globe with local times, list of world clocks (Edit / + to change the list).
- Seasons tab: current season, explanations for children, sun and moon times.
- Prayer tab: today's prayer times and the countdown to the next one; the bell icon turns on prayer-time notifications (asks for notification permission only then); monthly table and Ramadan timetable (shareable image).
- Tools tab: Alarm, Timer, Stopwatch, Pomodoro, Date calculator, then Settings (location, color theme, units, 12/24 hour, appearance) and About (website, contact, optional newsletter sign-up, privacy).
The app language follows the device language (Turkish, English, German, Azerbaijani, Arabic).

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES USED
- Apple WeatherKit: weather data (Weather tab, weather clock faces, globe). Apple Weather attribution and legal link are shown.
- Apple MapKit: the satellite globe in the World tab and on the clock screen.
- Apple frameworks on the device only: Core Location (approximate location, optional), EventKit (reading/adding calendar events, optional), UserNotifications and AlarmKit (alarms and prayer reminders), AVSpeechSynthesizer (spoken notification sounds generated on the device).
- Bumba mailing list service (bumbagroup.com): only if the user voluntarily enters an email address in About to receive news; the address is sent only for that purpose, with explicit consent.
- No other servers: prayer times, seasons and sun/moon data are calculated on the device with astronomical formulas; the holiday calendar is bundled with the app. No analytics, advertising or AI services.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The app works the same in all regions. Prayer times are calculated for whatever location the user selects anywhere in the world. The holiday calendar shows Turkey's official public and religious holidays for everyone (this is the app's focus). Weather depends on WeatherKit coverage. The interface is available in Turkish, English, German, Azerbaijani and Arabic.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not applicable. The app does not operate in a regulated industry and does not include protected third-party content. Prayer times are informational and calculated on the device with the method used by Turkey's Presidency of Religious Affairs (Diyanet); the app states that Diyanet's official times are authoritative. Holiday dates come from public official calendars.
