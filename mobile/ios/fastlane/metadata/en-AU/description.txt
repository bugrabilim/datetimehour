Time & Date brings the clock, widgets, weather, calendar, alarms and everyday tools together in one clean app. It's free, with no account, no ads and no tracking.

SET UP FOR YOU
• Five quick questions on first launch: weather, calendar, world clocks, seasons, prayer times. Your tab bar is built from your answers
• Sections you don't use stay hidden and send no notifications

CLOCK AND WIDGETS
• 21 clock styles and 25 analogue dials; full screen and a dim red night mode
• Add the weather, date, world clocks or the next prayer time under the clock
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets: clocks, world clocks, countdowns, Moon phase; Apple Watch complications

CALENDAR AND WEATHER
• Public holidays for Türkiye and 30 more countries, including Australia, plus religious and special days, next to the calendars on your phone
• Hijri calendar and converter
• Hourly and 10-day forecast, air quality, and a screen that changes with the weather

ALARM, SLEEP AND FOCUS
• Alarms that ring even in silent mode, with wake-up tasks such as maths, shaking or scanning a code
• Sleep plan, sleep sounds, smart alarm
• Pomodoro, study timer, app blocking while you focus

TOOLS
• Timer, stopwatch, interval training, chess clock, metronome, talking clock
• World clocks with a spinning globe, meeting planner, time and date calculators
• Seasons, Moon calendar, eclipses, the night sky, zodiac sign

PRAYER TIMES AND WORSHIP
• Prayer times anywhere, with 14 calculation methods (Diyanet, MWL, ISNA, Umm al-Qura and more) and Hanafi Asr
• All 81 provinces and 973 districts of Türkiye
• Notifications with an adhan sound, Ramadan timetable
• Qibla compass and nearby mosques
• The Quran in Arabic, duas, the 99 Names of Allah, a tasbih counter, prayer and fasting tracker, zakat calculator
• Greeting cards for Friday, Ramadan, Eid and holy nights

PRIVACY
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your location stays on your phone for prayer times; only for weather and nearby mosques is an approximate location sent to the service (Apple, Open-Meteo), never who you are. See and turn off every permission in Settings › Permissions.

In 18 languages. For iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.
