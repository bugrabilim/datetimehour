Time & Date brings the clock, date, prayer times, calendar and weather together in one clean app. No account, no ads, no tracking.

CLOCK
• 19 clock faces from our website: analog, digital, side by side, with calendar and weather
• Full-screen clock that keeps the screen on
• Choose what appears under the clock: next prayer, today's prayer times, next special day, world clocks, weather, sunrise

PRAYER TIMES
• Daily and monthly times for 81 provinces of Türkiye or your current location
• Notifications on time or in advance, with a spoken announcement
• Ramadan timetable as a shareable image

CALENDAR
• Public holidays, half days, religious and special days, school breaks in Türkiye
• The calendars on your phone (iCloud, Google, Outlook) on the same screen
• Add and delete events; add special days to your calendar

WEATHER
• Current, hourly and 7-day forecast from Apple Weather

TOOLS
• Alarm (rings even in silent mode), timer, countdown to a date
• Stopwatch, pomodoro, date calculator (including working days)
• World clocks with a spinning globe
• Seasons, equinoxes and solstices, moon phases

WIDGETS
• Next prayer, today's times, analog and digital clock, clock and date; also on the Lock Screen

Available in fifteen languages: English, Turkish, German, Azerbaijani, Arabic, Indonesian, Urdu, Persian, French, Russian, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Filipino and Thai.

Your location is used on your phone to calculate prayer times; only for the weather is an approximate location sent to Apple's weather service. Your calendars are read only if you allow it, and only on your phone.
