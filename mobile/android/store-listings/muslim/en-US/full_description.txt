Time & Date brings prayer times, the Qibla, a clock, a calendar, weather and everyday tools together in one clean app. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Prayer times and worship
• Prayer times for anywhere in the world, with 14 calculation methods (Diyanet, MWL, ISNA, Umm al-Qura, Karachi, Singapore and more) and Hanafi Asr
• All 81 provinces and 973 districts of Türkiye
• Notifications at prayer time or before it: the first phrase of the adhan, a short chime or a spoken announcement
• If you like, the phone switches to vibrate or Do Not Disturb at prayer time
• Ramadan timetable you can share as an image, with iftar and suhoor countdowns
• Qibla compass and nearby mosques on the map
• The Quran in Arabic, duas, the 99 Names of Allah, hadith, a prayer guide, a dhikr counter, a prayer and fasting tracker and a zakat calculator
• Hijri calendar and converter, religious days and holy nights, greeting cards for Friday, Ramadan and Eid

Set up for you
• Five quick questions on first launch build your tab bar. Sections you don't use stay hidden and send no notifications

Clock and widgets
• 21 clock styles and 25 analog dials, full screen and a dim red night mode
• Home screen widgets for the next prayer, today's prayer times, the Hijri date, clocks, world clocks, weather and the moon phase
• A Wear OS app with a tile and complications, an Android TV app, a screen saver, a floating clock and Quick Settings tiles

Calendar and weather
• Public holidays for Türkiye and 32 more countries, next to the calendars on your phone (Google Calendar included)
• Hourly and 14-day forecast, air quality and pollen, and a screen that changes with the weather

Alarm, sleep and focus
• Alarms that ring on the lock screen, with gradually rising volume and wake-up tasks such as math, shaking or scanning a code
• Sleep plan, sleep sounds and a smart alarm
• Pomodoro, study timer, interval training and app blocking while you focus

Tools
• Timer, stopwatch, chess clock, metronome, talking clock, meeting planner, time and date calculators
• Seasons, moon calendar, eclipses and the night sky

Privacy
No account, no ads, no tracking. Prayer times are calculated on your phone. Only for weather and nearby mosques is an approximate location sent to the service (Open-Meteo, OpenStreetMap), never who you are. You can see every permission in Settings › Permissions.

In 19 languages. Time & Date is a Bumba Life product. Web: time.bumba.tr
