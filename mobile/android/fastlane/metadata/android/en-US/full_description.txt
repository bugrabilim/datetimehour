Time & Date brings a clock, widgets, alarms, weather, a calendar and dozens of everyday tools together in one clean app. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Set up for you
• A few quick questions on first launch build your bottom bar from the sections you want
• Sections you don't use stay hidden and send no notifications; everything else is one tap away under All

Clock and widgets
• 21 clock styles and 25 analog dials; full screen, a dim red night mode for your bedside and a screen saver
• 27 home screen widgets: analog, digital and seconds clocks, world clocks, weather, moon phase, sunrise and sunset, countdowns, photos, year progress and more, with transparent and glass styles
• A floating clock over other apps and Quick Settings tiles
• A Wear OS app with tiles and complications, and a full-screen clock for Android TV

Alarm, sleep and focus
• Full-screen alarms on the lock screen with gradual volume, a sound library and backgrounds
• Wake-up tasks to turn them off: math, shaking, steps, squats, scanning a code, finding an object, memory or typing
• Smart alarm, sleep plan with bedtime reminders, sleep sounds and nature sounds
• Pomodoro with statistics and a to-do list, study timer, app blocking while you focus
• Interval training with voice cues, even with the screen off; save workouts to Health Connect

Weather
• Hourly and 14-day forecast, air quality and pollen, three weather models side by side
• A screen that moves with the weather: rain, snow, clouds, wind and lightning
• A morning weather summary and an optional weather notification

Calendar
• Month, week, day, agenda and year views next to the calendars on your phone
• Public holidays for 31 countries, including the US, and special days
• Hijri calendar and date converter

Tools
• Named timers, several stopwatches, countdowns with your own photos
• World clocks with a spinning globe, a map and a 24-hour dial; meeting planner; time and date calculators
• Chess clock, metronome, talking clock, morning journal, classroom tools, exam timer, zodiac sign

Sky and seasons
• Sunrise, sunset, golden and blue hour, moonrise and moonset, moon calendar
• Eclipses with maps, the night sky with planets and stars, sky event notifications
• The seasons explained for children

Prayer times and Qibla
• Prayer times anywhere with 14 calculation methods and Hanafi Asr; all 81 provinces and 973 districts of Türkiye
• Adhan notifications, prayer-time alarms, silencing your phone at prayer times, Ramadan timetable
• Qibla compass and nearby mosques
• The Quran in Arabic, duas, the 99 Names of Allah, tasbih counter, prayer and fasting tracker, zakat calculator, greeting cards

Privacy
Your location stays on your phone. Only for weather and nearby mosques is an approximate location sent to the service (Open-Meteo, OpenStreetMap), never who you are. See and turn off every permission in Settings › Permissions.

In 18 languages. For Android phones and tablets, Wear OS and Android TV.
